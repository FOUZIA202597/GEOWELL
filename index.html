    WQI Methodology & AI Logic

    The Water Quality Index (WQI) is a mathematical tool used to transform large quantities of water quality data into a single number which represents the overall quality of water. We use the Weighted Arithmetic Index method based on WHO Guidelines.

    1. Relative Weight Calculation (Wi)

    Each parameter is assigned a weight (wi) from 1 to 5 based on its relative importance in determining water quality for drinking purposes.

    2. Quality Rating (Qi)

    Qi = 100 * (Vi / Si). For pH: Qi = 100 * [(Vi - 7) / (Si - 7)].

    3. Final Index Calculation

    WQI = Σ (wi * Qi) / Σ wi
